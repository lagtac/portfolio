§ 04 — Rules

What the system refuses to do.

  • No colour without a word. Every status badge carries its label.
  • No motion on text being read. Reveals are 12 px and happen once.
  • No more than one accent. Vermilion means active, linked, or measured.
  • No number without a source. The case study cites the run or the report.
  • No body copy wider than 68 characters.
