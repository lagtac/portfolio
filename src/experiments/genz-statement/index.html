§ 01 — The manifesto

Four things I actually believe.

Not aspirational. Not a content strategy. The operating system.

  1. 01

    Rest is not a reward.

    I don't earn my rest by burning out first. Rest is the baseline, not the bonus. Nap, log off, come back sharper — the work will still be there, and so will I.

  2. 02

    Specific beats safe.

    A weird page that sounds like me beats a clean page that sounds like everyone. I'd rather be a little much than a little nothing. Safe is where personalities go to be deleted.

  3. 03

    Community over clout.

    Followers aren't friends and impressions aren't people. I'd trade a thousand views for one person who actually needed the thing I made. The group chat is the real algorithm.

  4. 04

    Make the weird thing.

    The unicorn stays. The bit stays. If it's too much for the feed, the feed was never the point. Make the thing that would have made fifteen-year-old you feel seen.
