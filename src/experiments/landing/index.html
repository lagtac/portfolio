§ 02 — Method

How I work.

Three habits, each with evidence in the case study.

01

Measure before you claim.

Every number on this page comes from a run, a test split, or a dated report. Where a result was bad, I say so.

02

Own the seams.

Four services work only if the contracts between them are explicit. The dataset export and the prediction API are the two seams I watch hardest.

03

Delete what doesn't earn its place.

I shipped a clinical gate engine, A/B tested it on 210 cases, found it touched about 0.6% of detections with no net benefit, and removed it — flag, API fields and all.
