§ 01

Nine years inside a Big Four firm.

I joined KPMG Greece in 2010 and left in 2018. My tenure was Content Manager on the marketing and digital team; the job was the firm's web estate and client projects.

That covered the Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) public website migration, SharePoint intranet migration, client and campaign sites, email and newsletter suites, SEO and analytics. I was responsible for maintaining and optimizing the Information Architecture of our online properties. I thoroughly supervised compliance and applied the global marketing strategy. I liaised with service owners for publishing. I edited the content, and measured the results.

The nine projects below are a selection made for evidence, not a complete list.
