§ 01

Nine years inside a Big Four firm.

I joined KPMG Greece in 2010 and left in 2018 as Content Manager on the marketing and digital team. The job was the firm's web estate and its client projects.

That covered the public site migration to Adobe Experience Manager and the intranet migration to SharePoint. It also covered client and campaign sites, the email and newsletter suites, and SEO. I set the information architecture and the publishing schedule, edited the content, and reported the analytics. Compliance sign-off and the firm's global marketing strategy ran through all of it.

The nine projects below are a selection made for evidence, not a complete list.

§ 03

Content, web, and marketing were one job.

The work ran from the words on a page to the template that rendered them and the analytics that measured the result. The same combination showed up in the later platform work: one person owning the interface, the data behind it, and the evidence that it held up.
