Most recent
Diagoradisa, Jul 2025 — Oct 2026

Since July 2025 I have been the only developer on Diagoradisa, a medical imaging platform for oral pathology. I built the annotation tool, the dataset pipeline, the model service, and the clinician app. That meant owning the deploy script, the database migrations, the API contract, and the test that catches the regression six months later.

Most of my work sits where the interesting failures are: the boundary between an annotation tool and a training dataset, or between a model server and the app a clinician actually uses. I like systems that can be inspected. Run IDs, dated reports, explicit contracts. Not because process is virtuous, but because that is what makes a bug findable after the context is gone.

I work across PHP and Laravel, Python and FastAPI, and the frontend. I am comfortable being the person who reads the changelog, reproduces the bad result, and then deletes the feature that did not earn its place. The case study is the long version of that sentence.

I live in Greece with my wife and son. I am looking for a fullstack role where the data is messy and the users are real.

How I got here

From 2010 to 2018 I was a content and web lead at KPMG Greece. I wrote the content, built the pages, and measured them: an intranet migration, a job board, campaign microsites, and an email suite. The tools were Umbraco, SharePoint, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and a lot of coordination.

Since 2025 the work has moved a layer down: Laravel services, Python model serving, dataset contracts, and the deploy scripts behind them. The through-line is the same. Write it, build it, measure it.
