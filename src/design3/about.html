How I got here

I studied philosophy in Thessaloniki, then a cross-discipline master's in cognitive science in Athens. The thesis was on methods in cognitive musicology: how you study the way people make sense of music.

My first job was EPIS, in 2004: ASP and PHP against SQL Server 2000, including the digital archive of the Greek National Opera.

Then nine years at KPMG Greece, 2010–2018, as Content Manager: the corporate web and intranet migrations, client sites, and email campaigns.

In 2019 I co-founded Faltso Art with Daphne Kammenou, a music and theatre group for children, and ran it until 2024: the programmes, the collaborators, the schools and parents' associations, the scores and arrangements.

From July 2025 until October 2026 I was the sole developer of a four-service clinical imaging platform: annotation tool, dataset pipeline, model server, clinician app. I built it with Claude Code and parallel agents, under a discipline that kept it safe: one contract per boundary, a test per claim, and a diff I read myself. The case study has the numbers and the three failures.

I live in Greece with my wife and son. I am looking for a fullstack role where the problem spans more than one layer and the evidence is expected to be real.
