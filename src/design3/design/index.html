Motion

GSAP with ScrollTrigger, and Lenis as the single smooth-scroll engine. Motion explains the system or the evidence; it never decorates. Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, Lenis and GSAP do not initialise and every final state renders immediately.

Accessibility

WCAG 2.1 A/AA is a hard floor at 390, 768, and 1440 px in both themes, checked with axe-core. Keyboard operability, visible focus, a skip link, semantic landmarks, and correct Greek language tagging are part of the system, not a pass at the end.
