# Publish Faltso Art photographs from the group's own site, with consent

**Status**: accepted

The project rules said "any photograph from `assets/faltso/`" must never
publish, and `AGENTS.md` says `assets/` never publishes. That rule was written
before the Faltso work had a page. The Faltso site has been public since 2022,
and its photographs are the only visual evidence of five years of work; without
them the page is a wall of text next to two projects that carry screenshots.

We decided to publish a small, chosen set of photographs from the group's own
site. They are copied into `src/design3/img/` as WebP, resized for the page, and
stripped of EXIF (including GPS). Nothing links to `assets/`; the publish
allowlist in `DEPLOY.md` names each file. The images show children, so consent
is the owner's to confirm; Dimitris confirmed the families' consent for these
photographs. No school name, enrolment, or revenue figure is published.

**Consequences**: the "any photograph from `assets/faltso/`" prohibition is
narrowed to "nothing from `assets/` publishes; photographs from the group's own
site may, through the `img/` allowlist". If consent is withdrawn, remove the
image, its allowlist line, and the reference on the page.
