# Portfolio

The static portfolio for Dimitris Chrysomallis, and the candidate builds that
compete to be it.

## Language

**Candidate**:
A design build under `src/designX/` that has not been chosen to publish.
_Not_: option, variant, draft

**Destination**:
The one candidate that ships. It is iterated in place; the others become
reference.
_Not_: winner, final, chosen one

**Reference**:
A build kept for comparison or as a record of what to avoid. `src/design1/` and
`src/design2/` hold this status.
_Not_: archive, old version

**Transition**:
The TypeScript and React work shown as dated work-in-progress, never as delivered
experience.
_Not_: pivot, upskilling

**Confidential project**:
A project whose role and one-line outcome may be stated, while its name, brand,
screenshots, and client details stay hidden until the client clears them.
_Not_: NDA project, hidden project
